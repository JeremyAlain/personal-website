About

I'm a founding member and research scientist at Apollo Research, where I work on the science of scheming: understanding whether, how, and why AI models become deceptive, and how we can measure and prevent that.

I studied at ETH Zürich (bachelor's and master's) and have long been interested in risks from AI and how to prevent them, and in research itself. Before helping to found Apollo, I worked with Ethan Perez at NYU for a bit over a year, on training language models with feedback.

Outside research

I'm Swiss and live in Zürich. Outside of work I play drums in an orchestra, and I do a lot of sports: the gym, running, and most things that involve moving. I also read a lot, fiction and non-fiction alike.
